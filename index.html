The AeroFill Breakthrough Protected Standby

Flat ₹3,200 Standby Unlocked by Friction

Airlines cannot discount seats openly because corporate flyers with expense cards will buy cheap tickets instead of full-fare ₹16,500 seats.

AeroFill solves this not through discount sales, but through 6 intentional physical frictions: middle seats only, 7kg backpack limit, ±90 min departure window, and GPS geofence within 15 km of the airport. Everyday travelers in emergencies happily accept these rules; corporate executives refuse them every time.

Act 4 • Operational Friction by Design

The 6 Travel Rules: Friction That Defends Yield

Instead of asking for salary slips or income proof, we introduce 6 physical friction hurdles. Students and distressed travelers happily accept them; corporate travelers expensed by their firms refuse them 100% of the time.

6/6 Rules Active
Friction Verification Engine Protected Mode
Simulate GPS geofencing, T-3h departure gates, and middle-seat constraints live.
Rule 01

15 km Airport Perimeter

Standby fares unlock strictly when the traveler's GPS is verified within 15 km of the departure airport.

Revenue Protection

Prevents corporate procurement desks from booking discounted standby tickets from downtown office towers.

Rule 02

T-Minus 3 Hour Gate

Standby inventory unlocks exactly 180 minutes before takeoff, long after scheduled commercial sales peak.

Revenue Protection

Executives require advance calendar certainty days prior and cannot gamble on last-minute gate uncertainty.

Rule 03

Flexible ±90m Window

Travelers select a 3-hour departure band rather than a specific flight; the airline balances seats across flights.

Revenue Protection

Corporate travelers with tight executive meetings and client presentations cannot accept flexible flight assignments.

Rule 04

Middle Seats Only (Row B & E)

Standby travelers receive rear unselected middle seats only, leaving coveted window and aisle seats for commercial fliers.

Revenue Protection

Physical seating comfort is the decisive psychological barrier that prevents corporate travelers from downgrading.

Rule 05

Instant Digital ID Verification

Students and emergency travelers verify eligibility in seconds through DigiLocker and India's ABHA health record network.

Revenue Protection

Tickets are strictly non-transferable and tied to verified Aadhaar/ABHA tokens, completely preventing black-market scalping.

Rule 06

7kg Backpack Only (Zero Check-in)

Strict carry-on backpack only. Checked baggage is prohibited to guarantee zero baggage-hold handling delays at departure.

Revenue Protection

Multi-day business executives who carry suit trolleys and garment bags cannot travel on standby rules.

Core Design Finding: In qualitative testing with 5 frequent flyers (2 corporate consultants and 3 students), 100% of corporate participants refused these 6 rules. Operational friction protects airline yield automatically without asking for salary verification.

Act 6 • Priority Medical Care

Project Sanjeevani: Dignity in Urgent Crisis

Holding ~3 emergency seats per flight for verified hospital crises at a fixed ₹3,200. Verified in under 90 seconds through ABHA digital health records.

Project Sanjeevani • Medical Standby Tier
72 BPM

When Minutes Count: Dignity Over Profit

Last-minute fares can be hardest on families facing a medical emergency. When a child is transferred for emergency surgery or an organ recipient gets a call, airlines charging ₹22,000 spot fares causes severe financial distress.

Under Project Sanjeevani, 14% of the standby pool on domestic routes is permanently earmarked for verified medical emergencies at an absolute flat cap of ₹3,200, paired with Class Alpha queue priority and automated Digi Yatra airside escort.

Outcome Honest Operational Trade-Off

Honest trade-off: The carrier forgoes surge pricing on these emergency seats, but permanently eliminates gate confrontation while covering marginal passenger costs.

Instead of capturing ₹18,000+ spot revenue from a desperate traveler, the carrier accepts ₹3,200—which still generates a healthy +₹1,366 marginal contribution above variable costs (₹820 jet fuel + ₹630 statutory airport security fee). It permanently removes reputational liability and gate confrontation while saving families an average of ₹18,800 during acute ICU transfers.

What I rejected and why

1. Post-Travel Reimbursement Claims

Rejected because families facing ICU deposits cannot afford to front ₹22,000 cash while waiting 30 days for airline corporate claim reviews.

2. Zero-Fare / Completely Free Emergency Seats

Rejected because commercial airlines operate on 2-4% net margins; ₹3,200 covers extra passenger jet fuel burn (₹820) and CISF airport fees (₹630), keeping operations economically sustainable.

3. Honor-System Checkboxes Without Verification

Rejected because self-declared medical discounts without third-party verification risk widespread exploitation, leading to disputes at the check-in counter.

What I rejected and why

1. Clickable Prototype Slides (InVision/PDF)

Rejected because passive slide decks cannot dynamically calculate multi-variable formulas when airline dispatchers challenge seat counts or fuel burn numbers.

2. Black-Box AI Price Predictors

Rejected because airline revenue management teams demand 100% transparent arithmetic formulas before trusting new inventory tools.

3. Unconstrained Multi-Class Sandbox

Rejected because allowing users to simulate business-class standby or unchecked baggage creates unrealistic operational scenarios that violate airline safety constraints.

Appendix • Policy & Regulatory Framework

Regulatory Compliance & Operational Standards

How AeroFill adheres to Indian Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) civil requirements, passenger rights charters, and airport turnaround SLA constraints.

DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M (Passenger Rights)

AeroFill operates strictly on voluntary standby. In the rare event of an aircraft down-gauge or irregular operations, standby travelers receive an instantaneous 100% refund in under 5 seconds or automatic priority rollover to the next departure window, with zero cancellation fees.

25-Minute Gate Turnaround Protection

To protect airline on-time performance (OTP), standby check-in closes strictly at T-45 minutes. Because standby fliers carry only 7kg backpacks, gate agents avoid hold-luggage loading queues, ensuring the aircraft pushes back on schedule.

Digi Yatra Biometric Integration

Standby boarding passes link directly to India's Digi Yatra facial biometric ecosystem. Passengers clear airport entry and CISF security gates using facial scans without paper boarding passes, preventing terminal congestion.
