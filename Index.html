Lesson 2: The Indian Miracle of the 25-Minute Turn

How IndiGo Turns an A320neo in 25 Minutes Flat

An airplane only generates revenue when its wheels are off the tarmac. On the ground at Delhi T3 or Mumbai T2, an aircraft is burning money every second. IndiGo became the world's most profitable low-cost airline by mastering the 25-minute quick turnaround.

Why Standby Must Be Hand-Luggage Only: Baggage loaders take 18 minutes to unload and load cargo holds. If a standby passenger shows up with checked luggage at T-50 minutes, the entire baggage belt halts, the flight misses its slot, and the airline incurs ₹3,50,000 in delay penalties. Hand-luggage only is the non-negotiable secret.

Lesson 3: Center of Gravity (CG Trim Physics)

Why Middle & Rear Seating Actually Saves Jet Fuel

Airplanes must balance on their wing aerodynamic center. An aircraft that is too heavy in the nose requires aerodynamic tail trim to push the nose up, which creates parasitic trim drag and burns extra Jet A-1 fuel!

AeroFill's algorithmic middle-seat and rear assignment naturally moves the aircraft Center of Gravity slightly rearward (towards the optimal aft trim boundary), reducing aircraft trim drag by up to 0.8% and saving fuel for the airline on every flight!
